Tenimon Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

This policy explains how Tenimon handles information when you use the app.

Data Collection

Tenimon does not require an account. The developer does not collect, store, sell, or use your personal data for advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Data Stored on Your Device

Connection names, hostnames or IP addresses, ports, usernames, preferences, trusted SSH host keys, and voice endpoint settings are stored locally on your device. SSH passwords, private keys, and voice service API keys are stored in the iOS Keychain.

Optional iCloud Sync

When you enable sync, all server configurations, passwords, and SSH private keys are encrypted on your device and uploaded together to your private iCloud database. The encryption key syncs through iCloud Keychain. Sync is off by default. Turning it off keeps local and cloud data. Conflicting changes require you to choose which complete copy to keep. An encrypted local backup of the most recently overwritten data can be restored in Settings. Terminal content, running sessions, trusted SSH host keys, and voice settings and API keys do not sync.

SSH and Mosh Connections

Terminal input and output travel directly between your device and the SSH or Mosh server you choose. The Tenimon developer cannot access your connection details, credentials, commands, or terminal content.

File, Photo, and Video Uploads

Uploads are optional. Tenimon requests camera or photo library permission when you choose those sources. Only files, photos, or videos you select, photos you take, or clipboard images you confirm are uploaded over encrypted SSH/SFTP to your connected server. Tenimon may keep temporary local copies for upload. The developer does not receive this content. Uploaded files remain on the server until you or its operator delete them.

Voice Input

Voice input is optional. Apple speech recognition uses on-device recognition by default; if you enable Apple online recognition, Apple may process your recording. With an OpenAI-compatible service, Tenimon sends your recording and API key directly to the HTTPS endpoint you select, which is OpenAI by default. That service handles the recording under its own privacy policy. Tenimon keeps a temporary recording on your device and normally deletes it after transcription or cancellation. You can review and edit the transcript before sending it to your connected server. The Tenimon developer does not receive your recordings, API keys, or transcripts.

Local Network

Tenimon requests local network access only when needed to connect to an SSH or Mosh server on your local network. Connection details are not sent to the Tenimon developer.

Clipboard

Tenimon reads the clipboard only when you explicitly choose to paste. Pasted content is sent to the server for the active connection.

Deleting Data

Deleting a connection removes its saved connection details and password. When sync is enabled, deletions also sync. The most recent overwrite backup may retain previous data until replaced. Trusted SSH host keys can be reviewed or removed separately in Settings under Trusted Hosts. You can remove a saved voice endpoint and its API key in Voice Settings.

Servers and Services You Choose

The operators of servers and voice services you choose may process data under their own privacy policies. Tenimon does not control those services.
